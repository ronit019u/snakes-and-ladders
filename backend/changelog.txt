Changelog – 2026-08-06

Added 10-multiple tile bonus trigger: introduced session.triggeredBonusTiles to track triggered tiles (10,20,...,90) per session; added checkTileBonusTrigger in playerHelpers.js to fire a bonus round when a player lands on a tile whose 10-multiple group hasn't been triggered; integrated the check in gameController.move (dice, quiz, rocket) and useItem after valid moves.


Changelog – 2026-08-05

Added services/playerHelpers.js: centralized buildPublicPlayerList and applyPlayerFinish to unify player list construction and finish logic, eliminating code duplication and adding completedAt field to public player data.

gameController.create / join: added completedAt: null to new player initialization to prepare for finish time tracking.

gameController.getState: switched to buildPublicPlayerList, so the returned leaderboard now automatically includes completedAt for frontend use.

gameController.move: added completedAt check to block finished players from moving; replaced hardcoded win logic with applyPlayerFinish when reaching tile 100.

gameController.useItem: added completedAt check for item users, completedAt validation for bomb targets, and replaced rocket win logic with applyPlayerFinish.

gameController.disconnect: switched to buildPublicPlayerList to maintain consistent leaderboard data format.

bonusController.submitBonusAnswer: added completedAt check to prevent finished players from participating; calls applyPlayerFinish when forward_boost reaches tile 100 with appropriate broadcast handling.

quizController.validateAnswer: added completedAt check to prevent finished players from answering questions.

adminCreateRoom preset loading: wrapped preset with getPreset() to fill in default fields, preventing crashes from manually edited incomplete presets in db.json.
